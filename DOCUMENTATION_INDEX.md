## 📌 Key Findings Summary

### What Playwright MCP Is
A tool that lets AI agents (Copilot, GPT-4, Claude) control Playwright browser automation through natural language prompts.

### When It's Good
- ✅ UAT test creation by business users
- ✅ Quick prototyping & exploration
- ✅ Template generation from test plans
- ✅ Interactive discovery workflows

### When It's Bad
- ❌ Production regression tests
- ❌ CI/CD automation
- ❌ Complex D365 workflows
- ❌ Anything requiring 100% reliability

### The Recommendation
**Keep your current framework.** It's production-grade and already proven.

**Optionally use MCP** for UAT scenarios or template generation (experimental only).

### Why Your Framework Wins
- $0 infrastructure costs
- 100% reliability (proven)
- No external dependencies
- 11/11 tests passing
- 100% data accuracy
- Works offline
- Perfect for CI/CD
